<h2>Disclosure of NEHR Information</h2>
<p>NEHR information should only be disclosed to other care providers within
the organisation who require it to provide patient care. As NEHR does not
contain patients' complete health records, healthcare professionals should
direct patients requesting NEHR information to view their health records
through HealthHub or contact the hospital or clinic that provided the relevant
treatment. Information from NEHR that has been incorporated into an institution's
medical records must be handled in accordance with the Healthcare Services
Act (HCSA), Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), and other applicable laws.</p>
<p></p>
<p>MOH will publish detailed guidelines on the appropriate access and use
of NEHR information as part of the HIB’s implementation.</p>
