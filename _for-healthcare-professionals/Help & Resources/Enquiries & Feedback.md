<h2>Feedback topics</h2>
<h3>Health Information Bill (HIB) Matters</h3>
<p>If you have any queries or feedback regarding the <strong>Health Information Bill (HIB)</strong>,
please fill up this <a href="https://go.gov.sg/hia-enquiries" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form.</a>
</p>
<h3>Healthcare Services Act (HCSA) Matters</h3>
<p>If you have any general queries related to HCSA, or if your queries are
specific to the General Regulations, Advertisement Regulations or price
transparency requirements under the HCSA, please visit <a href="www.go.gov.sg/askmoh-hcsa" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">www.go.gov.sg/askmoh-hcsa</a> to
find your answers.</p>
