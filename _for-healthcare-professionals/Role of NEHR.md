<h2>How NEHR should be used</h2>
<p>NEHR serves as an adjunct to existing clinical practice such as good history-taking
and physical examination which healthcare professionals can refer to if
required. It is not expected, nor required under HIB, to access NEHR for
every single consultation. NEHR and the information within should be used
in accordance with the relevant professional standards (e.g. Ethical Code
and Ethical Guidelines (ECEG)).</p>
