<h2>Access Restrictions</h2>
<p>Patients may limit access to NEHR. In such cases only basic information
(e.g. demographics and allergies) will be available to healthcare providers.
Healthcare professionals may ‘break glass’ in emergencies where accessing
NEHR is necessary to address an immediate threat to the patient's life.</p>
