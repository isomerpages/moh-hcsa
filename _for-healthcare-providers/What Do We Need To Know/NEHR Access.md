<h2>NEHR Access</h2>
<p>[Executive Summary] Healthcare providers will be required to implement
policies and practices to ensure NEHR information is only accessed, collected
and disclosed for the provision of a healthcare service</p>
<h2>Only select healthcare providers can access NEHR </h2>
<p>• HCSA licensees, retail pharmacy licensees and public agencies providing
healthcare services will be granted access to NEHR for patient care purposes
(referred to as Specified Users)</p>
<p>• Authorised personnel of Specified Users will also be granted NEHR access
for patient care eg registered doctors, nurses and pharmacists (referred
to as Authorised Individuals)</p>
