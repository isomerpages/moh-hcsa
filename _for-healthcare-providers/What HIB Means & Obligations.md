<p>Slide 16</p>
<p>Healthcare providers must ensure accurate and timely contribution to NEHR
and secure handling of data</p>
<p>Contribute</p>
<p>-Ensure the accurate, timely and complete contribution of health information
to NEHR</p>
<p>Protect</p>
<p>-Implement policies and practices to ensure appropriate access, collection
and disclosure of NEHR information eg audits and training</p>
<p>-Ensure a secure connection to NEHR eg meet cyber and data security requirements</p>
<p>Report</p>
<p>-Notify MOH of confirmed cybersecurity incidents and data breaches in
a timely manner</p>
