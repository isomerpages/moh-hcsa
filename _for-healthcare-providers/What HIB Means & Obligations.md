<h1>Healthcare Provider Responsibilities Under HIB</h1>
<p>Healthcare providers play a critical role in maintaining the integrity
and security of Singapore's National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) system.
Under the Health Information Bill (HIB), all licensed healthcare providers
must fulfil the following responsibilities.</p>
<hr>
<h2>1. Contribute</h2>
<p><strong>Ensure accurate, timely and complete contribution of health information to NEHR</strong>
</p>
<p>Healthcare providers are required to contribute key health information
to the NEHR as specified under the Health Information Bill. This means:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Submitting complete and up-to-date patient health records as required</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ensuring information is contributed within the timeframes specified in
the legislation</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Maintaining accuracy of all data entered into the system</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>These contributions enable healthcare providers to access necessary information
for informed clinical decisions and coordinated patient care.</p>
<hr>
<h2>2. Protect</h2>
<p><strong>Safeguard NEHR information through required security measures</strong>
</p>
<p>Healthcare providers must implement policies and practices to protect
patient information as required under the Health Information Bill:</p>
<p><strong>Appropriate Access and Handling</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Establish controls to ensure only authorised personnel access NEHR information
in accordance with their roles</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Conduct audits of access and system usage as required</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Provide training to staff on proper handling of health information</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Implement policies governing the appropriate collection, access and disclosure
of NEHR information</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Secure Technical Infrastructure</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Maintain secure connections to the NEHR system</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Meet the cyber and data security requirements specified by the Ministry
of Health</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Implement technical safeguards as required to prevent unauthorised access</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2>3. Report</h2>
<p><strong>Notify MOH of security incidents as required</strong>
</p>
<p>Healthcare providers must notify the Ministry of Health of confirmed cybersecurity
incidents and data breaches within the timeframes specified in the Health
Information Bill. This includes:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Notification of confirmed breaches or security incidents as required</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Providing information as specified to enable appropriate response</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cooperating with MOH as required under the legislationCooperating with
MOH as required under the legislation</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2><strong>Compliance</strong></h2>
<p>Healthcare providers must comply with all requirements under the Health
Information Bill. Non-compliance may result in enforcement action as provided
for in the legislation.</p>
<p>For detailed requirements and guidance, healthcare providers should refer
to the Health Information Bill and contact the Ministry of Health.</p>
