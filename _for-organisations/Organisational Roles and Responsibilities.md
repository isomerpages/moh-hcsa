<h1>Organisational Roles and Responsibilities</h1>
<h3>Responsibilities of the healthcare provider</h3>

<li>
<p>Objective 1</p>
</li>

<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MOH approval is required for entities’
access to NEHR.</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Entities should ensure that only
the personnel who require NEHR access for a HIA purpose are granted NEHR
access.</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Entities should ensure access to
NEHR is only for patients under entities’ care.</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Entities should notify Synapxe of
any personnel movements that affect NEHR access to ensure NEHR accounts
are removed in a timely fashion (e.g., personnel has left organisation
or changed role).</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Entity should educate their personnel
on appropriate use of NEHR information (e.g., by providing training, updating
SOP).</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Entity should establish internal
mechanisms to track and flag inappropriate accesses.</p>

<td rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Designate an individual to ensure
that they comply with cyber and data security requirements for IT systems
and medical devices under HIA</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ensure that the confidentiality,
integrity and availability of the health information are always protected,
and that health information is appropriately disposed of once the purpose
for which the health information is required is no longer being served
and the retention of the health information is no longer necessary for
legal or business purpose.</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Report cybersecurity incidents and
data breaches within the specified time period (2 hours), and an incident
report (within 14 days of initial notification).</p>
<p>o &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Note: No need to data breaches involving
non-health information, such as the loss of only financial data or account
login details. However, they may still be required to report such data
breaches to PDPC if it meets the PDPA's data breach notification criteria.</p>
