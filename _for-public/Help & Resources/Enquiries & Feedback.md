<h2>A)Health Information Bill (HIB)</h2>
<p>If you have any queries or feedback regarding the <strong>Health Information Bill (HIB)</strong>,
please fill up this <a href="https://go.gov.sg/hia-enquiries" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form.</a>
</p>
<h2>B)Health Information Bill (HIB)</h2>
<p>If you have any enquiries or feedback regarding the following categories,
please fill up this <a href="https://go.gov.sg/hia-enquiries" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form.</a>
</p>
<p><strong>Categorisation of Query/Feedback</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Cyber/Data Security Related (Requirements, Data Sharing etc.)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>NEHR Related (Access, Use, Contribution etc.)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Health Information Bill Related (Sensitive Health Info (SHI), Data Portability
etc.)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Request for Engagements, Briefings etc.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Others</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h2>Others</h2>
<p>If you have any general queries or feedback that are <strong>non-HIB related</strong>,
or if your queries are specific to Hospital/Polyclinic Matters, Regulatory
and Compliance Matters, please visit <a href="https://www.moh.gov.sg/feedback/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.moh.gov.sg/feedback/.</a>
</p>
