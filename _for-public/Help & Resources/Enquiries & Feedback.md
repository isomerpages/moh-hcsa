<h2>Health Information Bill (HIB)</h2>
<p>If you have any feedback or queries regarding the <strong>Health Information Bill (HIB)</strong>,
please fill up this <a href="https://go.gov.sg/hia-enquiries" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form.</a>
</p>
<h2>Others</h2>
<p>If you have any general queries/feedback that are <strong>non-HIB related</strong>,
or if your queries are specific to Hospital/Polyclinic Matters, Regulatory
and Compliance Matters, please visit <a href="https://www.moh.gov.sg/feedback/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.moh.gov.sg/feedback/.</a>
</p>
