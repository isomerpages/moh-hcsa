<h1>Baseline cybersecurity and data security measures</h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Healthcare providers as well as their IT vendors supporting connection
to NEHR must</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;Meet
cybersecurity and data security requirements for IT systems and medical
devices</p>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;Report
confirmed cybersecurity incidents and data breaches to MOH and affected
individuals in a timely manner</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Strict penalties will be imposed for any contraventions to the HIA requirements</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h1>NEHR has safeguards to protect against cyber-attacks</h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Third-party audits to ensure compliance with security standards for government-owned
systems.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Robust onboarding process to ensure systems used by healthcare providers
have met the appropriate connectivity and security requirements before
they are allowed to connect to NEHR.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h1>How we prevent improper access and use of your NEHR records</h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>There are built-in technical controls such as session timeout after a
period of inactivity, and controls to limit patient search.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Regular backend audits are performed to flag inappropriate accesses for
review.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>System Operator will conduct User Account Review periodically to remove
dormant accounts and ensure access granted are based on current business
needs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Patients can check which institutions/clinics has viewed their NEHR records
via access logs on Health Hub.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h1>What happens when a cybersecurity incident, data breach or unauthorised access to NEHR occurs?</h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Healthcare providers must report cybersecurity incidents or data breaches
to MOH.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;
An initial report of the confirmed cybersecurity incident or data breach
must be provided to MOH within 2 hours.</p>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;
The detailed incident report must be submitted after 14 days.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Healthcare providers are also required to notify the affected individuals
in the event of a notifiable data breach. Notifiable data breaches refer
to data breaches involving more than 500 individuals.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h1>Entities can be required to take corrective action</h1>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To minimise harm arising from non-compliances, MOH can direct contributors
to contribute health information in accordance with the Act, stop users
from accessing or collecting health information or require users to delete
health information in their possession.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;o&nbsp;&nbsp;
Failure to comply with a MOH Direction will result in an offence.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Strict penalties will apply to any authorised persons’ unauthorised access,
collection or use of NEHR information. There will also be enhanced penalties
in place to serve as a deterrent in certain scenarios:</p>
</li>
</ul>

<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>In the event of non-compliance or breach of HIA requirements, MOH has
the power to direct the System Operator to take a range of actions with
immediate effect to prevent or minimise harm to individuals arising from
the non-compliance/ breach. For example, the System Operator may limit
the data types that an user may access, suspend or remove an users’ access
to NEHR.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Min(H) may tap on emergency powers and direct healthcare providers to
perform non-cyber mitigating measures to respond to threats and incidents
affecting health information</p>
</li>
</ul>
