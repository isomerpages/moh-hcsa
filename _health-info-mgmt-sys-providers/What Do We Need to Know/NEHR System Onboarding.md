<h2>NEHR System Onboarding Framework</h2>
<p><strong>(For HIMS vendors intending to integrate with NEHR and not listed in <a href="https://cms.synapxe.sg/NEHR/Status%20of%20system%20integration%20with%20NEHR.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Category A</a>)</strong>
</p>
<p>The NEHR System Onboarding process ensures healthcare providers' systems
meet quality requirements, standards and data formats for NEHR integration.
This framework maintains data integrity and consistency within the NEHR
system.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>{Infographic with notes}</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The complete NEHR System Onboarding Process typically requires 30 weeks
(inclusive of SDD mapping), necessitating collaboration between healthcare
providers, HIMS vendors, and the NEHR team.</p>
