<h2>Disclosure of NEHR Information</h2>
<p>Only healthcare professionals involved in patient care may access NEHR,
and NEHR information may only be disclosed to other healthcare professionals
involved in caring for the same patient. As NEHR does not contain patients'
complete health records, healthcare professionals should direct patients
requesting NEHR information to view their health records through HealthHub
or contact the relevant healthcare provider that had provided the care
for which the information is being sought. Information from NEHR that has
been incorporated into an institution's medical records must be handled
in accordance with the Healthcare Services Act (HCSA), Personal Data Protection
Act (PDPA), and other applicable laws.</p>
