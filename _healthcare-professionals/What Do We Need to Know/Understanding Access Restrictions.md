<h2><strong>Emergency Access (“Break-Glass”)</strong></h2>
<p>When patients place Access Restrictions on their NEHR information, only
doctors can use the "break-glass" feature during genuine medical emergencies
when a patient's life or health is at immediate risk.</p>
<p><strong>Key Requirements:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Only for genuine medical emergencies</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cannot be used simply because a patient asks you to access restricted
information</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cannot be used for non-emergency situations, even when clinically helpful</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Must re-verify credentials and formally declare a medical emergency</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Consequences:</strong> Every “break-glass” instance is automatically
logged and audited. Inappropriate use will be investigated as a potential
breach and may result in referral to the Singapore Medical Council (SMC)
for disciplinary action.</p>
