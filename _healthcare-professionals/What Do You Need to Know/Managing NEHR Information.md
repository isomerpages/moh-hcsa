<p>As healthcare professionals, you play a vital role in safeguarding patient
information while using NEHR information to provide better patient care.
Here is what you need to know about your responsibilities when accessing
and using NEHR information.</p>
<h2><strong>Your Role with NEHR</strong></h2>
<p>NEHR does not replace good clinical practice and professional judgment,
which includes history-taking and physical examination. There is no need
for you to access NEHR for every single consultation.</p>
<p>NEHR is an adjunctive source of information for you if you require further
clinical information about the patients or if patients cannot recall their
information clearly. NEHR and the information within should be used in
accordance with relevant professional standards in their Ethical Code and
Ethical Guidelines.</p>
<p>Consider factors like whether you need additional information beyond what
you have gathered from patient interviews or examinations, or whether NEHR
information would be relevant to the current consultation.</p>
<h2><strong>When You Can Access NEHR Information</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Accessing NEHR information is not mandatory</strong>. You will
not be required to access NEHR for every consultation, and you do not need
to review every record when you do access it.</p>
<p>You may access NEHR information for:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Delivering patient care</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Conducting specific approved statutory medical examinations listed in <strong><u>HIA's Third Schedule</u></strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>When authorised by other written law or court order</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>When You Cannot Access NEHR Information</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Accessing NEHR information for employment or insurance purposes are strictly prohibited</strong>.
Severe penalties apply for unauthorised access, with higher penalties for
these prohibited uses.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>For Employment Screenings: </strong>Continue your current practice
- rely on history-taking, clinical assessment, and your own existing medical
records without accessing NEHR information.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>For Insurance Reports: </strong>Maintain your current practice
of using your medical records and patient interactions to prepare reports,
without accessing NEHR information. Prepare separate medical reports rather
than providing raw medical records or print-outs.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>If you refer to NEHR information during a medical examination, validate
or confirm relevant information with the patient during history-taking
and capture it in your own medical records with your clinical assessment.</p>
<h2><strong>Guidelines and Support</strong></h2>
<p>MOH is publishing comprehensive guidelines to support appropriate access
and use of NEHR information for all healthcare professionals, including
doctors, dentists, nurses, and allied health professionals. For more information,
click <strong><u>here</u></strong>.</p>
<p><strong><u>Report</u> </strong>inappropriate requests for NEHR information
for insurance purposes to MOH.</p>
