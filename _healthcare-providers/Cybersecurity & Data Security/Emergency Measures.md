<p>HIA empowers Minister for Health to implement emergency measures when
health information management systems (HIMS) face imminent threats that
could compromise patient data or disrupt healthcare services. Similar provisions
exist in the Infectious Diseases Act 1976 and Cybersecurity Act 2018.</p>
<p>Emergency powers address incidents including cyber-attacks, system failures,
physical infrastructure damage, or software malfunctions that threaten
healthcare service continuity. Powers focus on protecting health information
integrity regardless of threat origin.</p>
<p>When HIMS experience outages affecting multiple healthcare providers or
threatening service disruption exceeding 24 hours, Minister can direct
affected healthcare providers to implement specified mitigation or recovery
measures within defined timeframes.</p>
