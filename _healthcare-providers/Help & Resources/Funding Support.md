<h2>Funding Support for CSDS Implementation</h2>
<p>Eligible Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)&nbsp;that are subject
to HIA can enjoy&nbsp;<strong>co-funding support</strong>&nbsp;when you
sign up for&nbsp;CISOaaS&nbsp;(Chief Information Security Officer as-a-Service)
with&nbsp;DSaaS (Data Security as-a-Service)&nbsp;(MOH) as an add-on.&nbsp;</p>
<p>To sign up for these services:&nbsp;</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>SMEs that are subject to&nbsp;HIA&nbsp;and eligible for funding support:
Please&nbsp;identify&nbsp;your choice of&nbsp;CISOaaS&nbsp;consultant and/or
package and sign up at&nbsp;<a href="https://services2.imda.gov.sg/CTOaaS/Category/csa_1/cybersecurity-consultancy" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">IMDA's&nbsp;CTOaaS&nbsp;portal</a>.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Organisations that are subject to&nbsp;HIA&nbsp;and not eligible for funding
support: Please approach your choice of&nbsp;CISOaaS&nbsp;consultant directly
- see (b)(iii) above for the "CSA&nbsp;CISOaaS&nbsp;Providers Listing".&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) provides financial support for
eligible SMEs adopting cybersecurity solutions. Please visit <a href="https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">GoBusiness&nbsp;Gov Assist</a> for
the list of pre-approved solutions. You may apply for PSG through the 
<a href="https://www.apply.gov.sg/grants/business" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Business Grants Portal</a>.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Licensees that are members of the National Council of Social Services
(NCSS) should refer to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ncss.gov.sg/our-initiatives/tech-and-go/funding-support/social-service-agencies---consultancy-subsidy" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">NCSS’s Tech-and-GO! consultancy programme</a>.</p>
<h2>Funding Support for HIMS Adoption</h2>
<p>MOH is planning to launch one-off funding support for those mandated to
contribute to NEHR, to support adoption of NEHR-compatible systems. More
details will be published in due course.</p>
