<h2><strong>What to Do When You Encounter a Cybersecurity Incident or a Data Breach</strong></h2>
<p>You must report security incidents involving health information or systems
to MOH swiftly (as shown below), including:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Cybersecurity incidents (e.g. ransomware, malware, system attacks)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Data breach incidents involving health information that cause or are likely
to cause significant harm to individuals or affect or are likely to affect
500 or more individuals</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Unauthorised access or disclosure related to NEHR</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Fast reporting helps prevent further harm, protects patient information,
and allows MOH to provide guidance across the healthcare sector.</p>
<p>When you confirm a reportable incident involving cybersecurity or data
breach, notify MOH of the incident via our <a href="https://go.gov.sg/hia-incident-reporting-form" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form</a>.</p>
<p>If you would like to report a NEHR incident, you can use this separate
<a href="https://go.gov.sg/nehr-incident-reporting-form" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">online form</a>.</p>
