<h2>Ensuring Data Portability</h2>
<p>When you switch from one HIMS to another, you must <strong>maintain access to patient records and service recipient data</strong> for
safety and continuity of care. This requires accurate and complete data
migration between HIMS.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.healthinfo.gov.sg/files/COP_for_Data_Portability_1_0.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Code of Practice for Data Portability</a>,
developed by MOH in consultation with relevant stakeholders, sets out MOH's
minimum expectations for Data Migration.</p>
<h2>Objectives and Best Practice</h2>
<p>This Code sets out practices that HIMS providers must comply with when

<h2>Contractual Agreements with HIMS Providers</h2>
<p>You may outsource certain tasks or functions to your HIMS providers. Both
parties remain accountable for HIA compliance within their respective roles.</p>
<p>You are recommended to establish clear contractual agreements with HIMS
providers. These agreements could specify:</p>
<p>1. Purposes of health information processing</p>
<p>2. Duration and nature of health information processing, including any
data retention or disposal requirements</p>
<p>3. Requirements and incident response procedures that vendors could abide
by</p>
<p>4. Service management expectations</p>
<p>5. Expectations on data portability and transfer of health data to a new
HIMS provider if so required</p>
<p>You are advised to consult your legal advisers to address these points
in contracts, considering organisational business and operational needs.</p>
