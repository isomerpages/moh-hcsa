<h2>Definition of Healthcare Providers</h2>
<p>Healthcare providers include:</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Healthcare Services Act (HCSA) Licensees</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Public and Private
hospitals</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Outpatient medical
and dental clinics</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Clinical and radiological
laboratories</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Retail pharmacy licensees</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Public agencies providing healthcare
services (eg SAF and SCDF)</p>
<h2>Key Responsibilities for Healthcare Providers</h2>
<p>Healthcare providers have essential obligations in maintaining the integrity
and security of the National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) system. These
responsibilities encompass contribution, protection, and reporting requirements.</p>
<h3>Contributing to NEHR</h3>
<p>Healthcare providers must ensure the accurate, timely and complete contribution
of health information to NEHR. This obligation supports continuity of care
across the healthcare system and enables healthcare professionals to access
comprehensive patient information when needed.</p>
<h3>Protecting Health Information</h3>
<p>Healthcare providers are required to implement policies and practices
to ensure appropriate access, collection and disclosure of NEHR information.
This includes conducting regular audits and providing training to staff
on proper handling of health information.</p>
<p>Healthcare providers must also ensure a secure connection to NEHR by meeting
cyber and data security requirements. Whilst the Ministry of Health (MOH)
recognises that GP clinics and smaller healthcare organisations may have
limited resources and administrative capacity, these security measures
are essential to protect patient data. MOH will provide guidance and support
to assist healthcare providers in meeting these requirements.</p>
<h3>Reporting Incidents</h3>
<p>Healthcare providers must notify MOH of confirmed cybersecurity incidents
and data breaches in a timely manner. Prompt reporting enables swift action
to mitigate risks and protect patient information.</p>
<h3>Medico-legal Guidance</h3>
<p>MOH will provide clarity on the medico-legal obligations related to contributing
to, accessing, and sharing health information through NEHR. This guidance
will address concerns raised by healthcare providers regarding their legal
responsibilities.</p>
