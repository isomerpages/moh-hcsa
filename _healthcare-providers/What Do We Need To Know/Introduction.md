<p>Healthcare providers subject to HIA requirements include:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Healthcare Services Act (HCSA) licensees: public and private hospitals,
outpatient medical and dental clinics, clinical and radiological laboratories</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Retail pharmacy licensees under Health Sciences Authority (HSA)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Public agencies providing healthcare services (Example: Singapore Armed
Forces Medical Corps and Singapore Civil Defence Force Medical Services)</p>
</li>
</ul>
