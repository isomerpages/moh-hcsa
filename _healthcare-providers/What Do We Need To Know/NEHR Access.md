<h2>NEHR Access</h2>
<p>Healthcare providers will be required to implement policies and practices
to ensure NEHR information is only accessed, collected and disclosed for
the provision of a healthcare service.</p>
<h3>Only select healthcare providers can access NEHR</h3>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; HCSA licensees, retail pharmacy
licensees and public agencies providing healthcare services will be granted
access to NEHR for patient care purposes (referred to as <strong>Specified Users</strong>)</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Authorised personnel of Specified
Users will also be granted NEHR access for patient care eg registered doctors,
nurses and pharmacists (referred to as Authorised Individuals)</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <strong>Specified Users</strong> and <strong>Authorised Individuals</strong> are
detailed in <strong>Second Schedule</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Minister may approve other entities
to access NEHR for prescribed purposes e.g. community health providers
→ referred to as <strong>Approved Users</strong>
</p>
<p>➢<strong>Approved Users</strong> and their <strong>Authorised Individuals</strong> must <strong>be published in Gazette</strong>
</p>
<h3>NEHR users must comply with requirements</h3>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Only access NEHR for <strong>permitted purposes</strong>,
and <strong>not access NEHR for employment or insurance purposes</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Implement <strong>policies and practices to ensure appropriate access</strong> to
NEHR e.g. training Authorised Individuals on when access is allowed</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <strong>Comply with conditions and restrictions</strong> e.g.
only accessing NEHR of individuals they provide care to</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <strong>Comply with MOH’s directions</strong> e.g.
to delete or destroy copies of NEHR information in their possession</p>
