<h2>What happens if you encounter a cybersecurity incident or a data breach?</h2>
<p>You are responsible for <strong><em>timely incident reporting</em></strong> to
MOH. Prompt identification, assessment and reporting allow you to take
swift action to mitigate potential harm and safeguard patient health information.
Reporting also enables MOH to provide necessary guidance, investigate incident
causes, and implement preventive measures across the healthcare sector.</p>
<p>The HIA mandates reporting of specified cybersecurity incidents and health
data breaches.</p>

<h2>Step-by-Step Incident Response Process</h2>
<p><strong>1.&nbsp;Investigate Suspected Incidents</strong>
</p>
<p>When you suspect a possible incident, verify it immediately through investigation.
If you work with your HIMS provider, inform them promptly so that all parties
can respond swiftly.</p>
<p><strong>2.&nbsp;Confirm and Contain</strong>
</p>
<p>Once investigation confirms an incident has occurred:</p>
<p>Secure systems and limit further exposure</p>
<p>Assess the extent of the incident</p>
<p>Determine whether it meets the criteria for notifiable incidents to MOH</p>
<p><strong>3.&nbsp;Report to MOH</strong>
</p>
<p>If the incident is assessed to be notifiable, submit initial notification
within 2 hours to MOH through [form linked here], providing information
about the nature of the incident, potential impact, and immediate actions
taken.</p>
<p><strong>4.&nbsp;Submit Detailed Report</strong>
</p>
<p>Within 14 days of initial notification, provide a detailed incident report
outlining how the incident was managed and measures being taken to prevent
recurrence.</p>
<p><strong>5.&nbsp;Notify Affected Individuals</strong>
</p>
<p>Notify affected individuals whose health information may have been compromised,
concurrently with or as soon as practicable after notifying MOH, if the
incident causes or is likely to cause significant harm.</p>
<p>Do consider your obligations under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)
and/or other regulatory bodies as different notification requirements and
timelines may apply.</p>

<h2>Building Strong Incident Response Capabilities</h2>
<p>To ensure compliance and effective incident management, you should:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Establish and regularly update internal response processes</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Train all staff in incident identification and reporting procedures</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Maintain clear communication channels with your HIMS provider for support</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Implement preventive measures based on lessons learned</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Conduct regular audits of your response capabilities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Stay informed about evolving cyber and data security threats and regulatory
requirements</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
