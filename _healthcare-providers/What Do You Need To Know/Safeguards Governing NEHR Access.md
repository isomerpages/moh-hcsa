<p>We understand that patients trust you to keep health information about
them confidential, which is why HIA includes strong legislative protections
alongside technical controls that tightly regulate who can access what
information. However, you will need to play your part too in maintaining
these security standards.</p>

<td rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<p>Strong legal protections are in place:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Only authorised healthcare professionals can access NEHR information</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Healthcare providers must train their staff on proper NEHR use</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Regular audits ensure appropriate access to NEHR information</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Strict penalties apply for <strong><em>unauthorised access</em></strong> to
NEHR information</p>
</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td rowspan="1" colspan="1">
<p>Multiple technical controls protect NEHR information:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Healthcare professionals only see the types of information they need for
their role</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>System limits on how many patient records can be accessed within a certain
timeframe</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Regular automated monitoring to detect suspicious access patterns</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Progressive rollout of additional security measures</p>
</li>
</ul>
</td>
