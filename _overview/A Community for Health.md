<h2>A Community for Health</h2>
<p>The Health Information Bill (HIB) establishes a comprehensive framework
that enables Singapore's healthcare system to better serve our community
through the responsible sharing of health information. This legislation
recognises that effective healthcare extends beyond individual treatment
to encompass public health initiatives and community-wide care coordination.</p>
<h2>Leveraging Health Information for Public Benefit</h2>
<p>Given the wealth of information in NEHR, the Minister may approve use
of NEHR information for public health or public interest purposes, with
safeguards in place. This provision allows for 2 distinct types of information
sharing:</p>
<p>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Sharing of identifiable NEHR information
for public health purposes and;</p>
<p>2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Sharing of non-identifiable NEHR information
for public interest purposes.</p>
<p>When sharing identifiable information, the Minister may impose conditions
and restrictions to limit access to relevant NEHR information only, such
as granting special accounts that limit NEHR access to certain records
or data types only.</p>
<p>To ensure responsible use, persons who gain access to NEHR information
for these purposes must comply with certain obligations, including ensuring
information obtained is only used or disclosed for the purpose approved
by the Minister.</p>
<h2>Enabling Community Healthcare Partnerships</h2>
<p>The HIB provides legal basis for "A Community for Health" by enabling
the sharing of identifiable health information outside NEHR with controls
in place.</p>
<p>Sharing relevant information outside of NEHR is allowed if the following
3 conditions are met:</p>
<p>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Information sharing is limited to between
specified entities. To start with key public healthcare stakeholders, including
AIC, healthcare clusters and public agencies. In future, this could extend
to other private and community healthcare partners.</p>
<p>2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Information sharing is restricted to
specified use cases, with use cases scoped to situations that support continuity
of care and outreach to benefit target populations.</p>
<p>3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Information sharing is confined to specified
datatypes or sets for each use case.</p>
<p>The HIB uses "relevant information" as use cases may entail sharing health
information plus other identifiable data of an individual or his or her
caregiver. This will be defined through subsidiary legislation, with prescribed
data types scoped to basic identification data and health risk indicators.</p>
<p>Through these carefully structured provisions, the Health Information
Bill creates a foundation for collaborative healthcare that protects individual
privacy whilst enabling our healthcare community to work together more
effectively for the benefit of all Singaporeans.</p>
