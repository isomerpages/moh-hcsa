<h4>Authorised healthcare professionals may access your NEHR records </h4>
<p>• Summary of the types of users who can access NEHR</p>
<p>• Access to NEHR records only for the provision of healthcare service
and community health service</p>
<p>• Access to NEHR records not allowed for</p>
<p>o Any employment purpose (except for statutory medical examinations prescribed
under the HIA); or</p>
<p>o Assessment of insurability.</p>
<p>• MOH has developed a set of guidelines to guide healthcare professionals
on the appropriate use and access to NEHR records.</p>
<h4>How do I know who has assessed my NEHR records?</h4>
<p>• Patients can check which institutions/clinics has viewed their NEHR
records via access logs on Health Hub.</p>
<h4>Granting others access to your HealthHub records</h4>
<p>• Link to HealthHub’s FAQs on Am I able to decide who has access to my
records and results?</p>
<h4>Can I refuse access to or sharing of my NEHR records?</h4>
<p>• Individuals can (i) select which NEHR Users (e.g. institutions, clinics,
pharmacies, etc) can access their NEHR information; or (ii) block access
to all NEHR Users.</p>
<p>• The opt-out will apply only to Users who are accessing the NEHR for
the purpose of patient care.</p>
<h4>What happens after I exercise my access restriction rights?</h4>
<p>• Even if an individual has opted-out:</p>
<p>o A basic set of essential information will be viewable by all NEHR Users,
to minimise impact to care. These are allergies and vaccinations records
(together with key demographic information).</p>
<p>o All their data can be accessed in a life-and-death medical emergency
(“Break glass”).</p>
<p>o Contribution of health information to NEHR is required even if an individual
has opted out. This ensures that there are no gaps in their NEHR record
should they choose to opt back in later.</p>
<p>• Sharing of opted out NEHR information will be allowed under the following
circumstances:</p>
<p>o Where required or permitted by other written law (e.g. the PSGA or CPC).</p>
<p>o Where requested on public health grounds and approved for sharing by
the Minister for Health or his delegated authorised officer.</p>
<h4>I’ve changed my mind and want to remove the access restriction</h4>
<p>• Can opt back in via HealthHub</p>
