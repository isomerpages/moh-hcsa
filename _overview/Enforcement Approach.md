<h2>MOH will take a calibrated approach toward enforcement, enabled by a range of levers in HIB</h2>
<p>1. MOH will <strong>work with healthcare providers to resolve </strong>non-compliance
(e.g. technical challenges leading to non-contribution; log-in using wrong
account)</p>
<p>2. If necessary, MOH <strong>may issue directions </strong>to require the
healthcare provider to take steps take to remedy or prevent recurrence</p>
<p>3. <strong>Calibrated enforcement will be adopted, should providers fail to respond to directions.</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Letters of advice/warning</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Suspension of contribution or access to NEHR</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Offers for composition of offences</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>4. For <strong>persistent or deliberate non-compliance</strong>, we will
prosecute.</p>
