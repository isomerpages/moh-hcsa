<p>MOH will take a calibrated approach to ensure compliance, recognising
that there will be instances where non-compliance issues arise from genuine
challenges rather than deliberate disregard.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>First, when non-compliance occurs, for example for technical difficulties
with contribution systems or staff accidentally logging in with wrong accounts.
MOH will work collaboratively with healthcare providers to resolve these
issues and provide the support they need.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Second, if the collaborative approach doesn't resolve the matter, MOH
may issue directions requiring the healthcare provider to take specific
steps to remedy the situation or prevent recurrence.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Third, should healthcare providers fail to respond to these directions,
the HIA provides for a range of enforcement actions. This includes for
example letters of advice or warning or suspension of contribution or access
to NEHR, depending on the circumstances.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Fourth, for persistent or deliberate non-compliance where healthcare providers
are clearly disregarding their obligations, we will prosecute. Our penalties
are calibrated based on both the nature and impact of the offence, ensuring
the response is proportionate to the situation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
