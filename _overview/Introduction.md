<h1>Objectives of the Health Information Act (HIA)</h1>
<p>The Act is structured into Three Objectives:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Objective 1 provides the legislative basis for (i) mandatory contribution
of selected health information to the NEHR, and (ii) access to the NEHR
for patient care and other specified purposes.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Objective 2 enables sharing of identifiable non-NEHR health information
between prescribed entities (e.g., clusters, public agencies) for specified
use cases.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Objective 3 sets out the Cybersecurity and Data Security Requirements
imposed on HIA entities1 and providers of Health Information Management
Systems (HIMS)</p>
</li>
</ol>
