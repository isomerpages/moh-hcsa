<h3>Objectives of the Health Information Act (HIA)</h3>
<p>The Act is structured into Three Objectives:</p>
<p>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Objective 1 provides the legislative
basis for (i) mandatory contribution of selected health information to
the NEHR, and (ii) access to the NEHR for patient care and other specified
purposes. &nbsp;</p>
<p>2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Objective 2 enables sharing of
identifiable non-NEHR health information between prescribed entities&nbsp;(e.g.,
clusters, public agencies) for specified use cases.</p>
<p>3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Objective 3 sets out the Cybersecurity
and Data Security Requirements imposed on HIA entities1 and providers of
Health Information Management Systems (HIMS)</p>
