<h2>Responding to Threats</h2>
<h3>Minister may implement emergency measures during critical events and direct healthcare providers and Health Information Management System (HIMS) providers to take measures to respond to threats affecting health information</h3>
<p>• HIB enables Minister(Health) to take emergency measures during critical
events. These powers can only be used during occurrence/likely occurrence
of critical event that may result in loss or compromising of health information;
and causes/likely to cause</p>
<p>- Serious or irreversible harm to health, safety or welfare of a substantial
number of individuals;</p>
<p>- Serious and imminent threat to the public health; or</p>
<p>- Severe disruption to availability and provision of healthcare service
in Singapore</p>
<p>• Minister may direct healthcare providers to take any measure or comply
with any requirements necessary to prevent or remedy the loss or compromising
of health information</p>
<p>- Healthcare providers could be directed to replace lost/compromised information</p>
<p>- They could also be directed to direct its partners/HIMS providers to
support the above</p>
