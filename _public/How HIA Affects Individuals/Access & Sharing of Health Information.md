<h2>Access of Health Information in NEHR</h2>
<p>Authorised healthcare professionals may access individuals' health information
in NEHR.</p>
<p>NEHR may only be accessed by authorised healthcare professionals to provide
direct patient care for you. Other circumstances where your NEHR may be
used include when information from the NEHR is required to be provided
under other written laws.</p>
<p>Healthcare professionals are not allowed to access your NEHR for employment
or insurance purposes (except for statutory medical examinations listed
in Third Schedule of HIA).</p>
<p>For more information on how you can control access to your health information,
see Safeguards &amp; Controls.</p>
<h2>Sharing of Health Information Beyond NEHR</h2>
<p>The HIA enables sharing of health information only if it fulfils these
requirements:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The parties who are permitted to share and receive your health information
must be listed in the HIA</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The specific use case for data sharing must be listed in the HIA</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Only the specific data types that are prescribed under the HIA can be
shared</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Your health information can be shared only for the use case listed in
the HIA</p>
</li>
</ol>
