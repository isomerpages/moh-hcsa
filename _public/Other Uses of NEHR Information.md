<p>As Singapore's national repository for key health information, NEHR serves
a broader purpose beyond individual patient care – it helps us shape national
health policies and conduct research that benefits all Singaporeans.</p>
<h2><strong>When We Share NEHR Information</strong></h2>
<p>HIA allows us to share <em>identifiable </em>NEHR information when needed
for public health purposes, and <em>anonymised</em> NEHR information for
broader public interest purposes.</p>
<p>For example, if there is a major drug contamination incident, MOH can
quickly access NEHR information to identify patients who have been prescribed
the affected medication. We can then share this information with relevant
healthcare institutions so they can immediately contact these patients,
advise them to stop taking the drug, and seek medical attention.</p>
<p>HIA will not prevent sharing NEHR information as required or permitted
under other laws. For instance, NEHR information may be required under
the Criminal Procedure Code 2010 to facilitate criminal investigations
by the Police, or by the Communicable Diseases Agency under the Infectious
Diseases Act 1976 for outbreak investigations and contact tracing.</p>
<h2><strong>Our Careful Assessment Process</strong></h2>
<p>We do not take these requests lightly. For every request we receive, MOH
carefully assesses whether the NEHR information is appropriate and necessary
for the purpose, considering factors like whether alternative information
would work, and whether anonymised or aggregated data would suffice.</p>
