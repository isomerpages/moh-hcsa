<h2>Technical and Security Controls</h2>
<p>NEHR access* is limited to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Provision of patient care</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Community health services</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Public health purposes approved by MOH</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Technical controls include:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Access limited to data types required for clinical duties (e.g. retail
pharmacists do not have access to radiological images)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Built-in controls limiting access to individuals registered in healthcare
providers' care and preventing inappropriate access such as multiple/bulk
searches</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Regular backend audits to flag inappropriate access</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The HIA includes penalties to deter unauthorised access. Healthcare providers
may only access NEHR information of patients registered with them. Only
personnel directly involved in patient care are granted NEHR access, and
providers must update NEHR when personnel roles change.</p>
<p>MOH can direct healthcare providers to take corrective actions such as
stopping unauthorised access and destroying health information collected
in an unauthorised manner.</p>
<p>NEHR is protected against cyber-attacks through comprehensive security
measures, regular audits, vulnerability scans and penetration tests to
ensure compliance with government security standards. Systems connecting
to NEHR undergo robust onboarding processes to meet connectivity and security
requirements.</p>
