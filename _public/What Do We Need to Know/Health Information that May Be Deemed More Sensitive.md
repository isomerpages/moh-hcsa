<h2>Safeguards for Health Information that May Be Deemed More Sensitive</h2>
<p>We recognise that patients have concerns about accessing certain types
of health information that may be deemed more sensitive and the need for
stronger safeguards around this information.</p>
<p>Such health information includes sexually transmitted infections, delusional
disorders and schizophrenia. When it comes to diagnoses and test results
for these conditions, we have put additional protective measures in place.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>First, we have restricted who can see this information. Only specific
healthcare professionals directly involved in a patient's care can access
it, and this depends entirely on their role. Take a nurse working in a
psychiatric ward, for example – they will have access to psychiatric information
for the patients they are caring for, but not beyond what is necessary
for their work.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Second, we have added an extra security step. Before any authorised healthcare
professional can view this information, they must go through a double log-in
process. This means they must verify their credentials again before gaining
access. It is designed to ensure the information is only accessed when
genuinely needed and prevents any accidental viewing.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Synapxe monitors access to health information that may be deemed more
sensitive closely. Rest assured, any unauthorised access to such NEHR information
will be thoroughly investigated, and appropriate penalties will be imposed
where necessary.</p>
