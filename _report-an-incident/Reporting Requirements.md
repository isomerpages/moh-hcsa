<h4>What to report?</h4>
<p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
A notifiable cybersecurity incident, i.e., a cybersecurity incident in
respect of any applicable computer or computer system under the control
of the healthcare provider, which is prescribed, e.g. ransomware</p>
<p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
A notifiable data breach that (is likely to) result in significant harm
to an affected individual or is (likely to be) of a significant scale</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; Breach involves sensitive health information, or</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; ≥500 individuals affected</p>
<h4>Reporting timelines</h4>
<p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
Healthcare providers are required to:</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; Provide an initial notification to MOH within 2 hours of
a confirmed incident</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; Provide an incident report within 14 days of initial notification</p>
<p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
IT vendors are required to notify the HIA entity of suspected data breach
without delay</p>
